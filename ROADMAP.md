# Roadmap

The reliability roadmap is gate-driven and has no promised dates. The detailed
task graph and acceptance evidence live in
[`docs/project-reliability/BACKLOG.md`](docs/project-reliability/BACKLOG.md).

1. **G1 — foundations:** renderer/package feasibility, reproducible dependencies,
   community policies, and identity research.
2. **G2 — deterministic core:** safe offline resources, output integrity, fixed
   render profile, and visual/structural regression harness.
3. **G3 — platform candidate:** CLI, GUI, themes, and native packaging validated
   end to end. Windows x64 is the first mandatory lane.
4. **G4 — official readiness:** signed artifacts where supported, checksums,
   SBOM, provenance, release evidence, and factual download documentation.
5. **Independent final audit:** re-score the unchanged baseline rubric and
   publish residual risks.

Linux and macOS are independent future gates and are not advertised until their
native evidence passes. Priorities may change when evidence uncovers risk; a
roadmap item is not a release promise.
