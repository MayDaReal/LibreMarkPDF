# Code of conduct

LibreMark PDF contributors must make participation respectful, safe, and
welcoming regardless of experience, identity, background, language, or ability.

Expected behavior includes constructive technical feedback, respect for user
privacy, clear disclosure of uncertainty, and patient collaboration across
English and French. Harassment, threats, discrimination, sexualized attention,
doxxing, deliberate exposure of private documents or credentials, and sustained
disruption are not acceptable.

Report conduct concerns privately through the repository owner's contact
channel or, when no private channel is published, ask a maintainer in a public
issue to provide one without describing the incident. Maintainers will limit
information to people needed for a fair review, avoid conflicts of interest,
and may warn, restrict, or remove participants according to severity.

This policy applies in project spaces and when someone represents the project.
Enforcement decisions may be appealed to a maintainer who did not make the
original decision when one is available.
